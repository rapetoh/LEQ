## Product rules I will apply as defaults unless you say otherwise

The block below is the plan's text, verbatim. Each rule is a proposal awaiting objection; silence means it ships as written. Where a rule maps to a configuration key, the key is named after the block so Rebecca can change the value without a release.

> ## Product rules I will apply as defaults unless you say otherwise
>
> These are not in the documents. Each ships as a configuration value where it can, and is listed in `docs/OPEN-INPUTS.md`.
>
> - Gratuit "une étape par jour" = one validated step per day; a failed step may be retried the same day up to a configurable cap (3), and the remediation exercise does not count as a step.
> - An Arena take, a duel take or a debate validates the streak day like any recording (ch. 6: "ce qui valide une journée, c'est d'enregistrer").
> - Arena anonymity holds during votes; names appear on the podium only for users who opted in (G3 "Publier sous mon prénom"), pseudonym "Voix N" otherwise.
> - A duel closes when both have spoken or at 48 h; with one take only it expires without verdict and the inviter is told. The invitee does not hear the inviter before recording.
> - Debate resume window 30 minutes; the cap clock pauses while interrupted.
> - Geo filter uses a region list (French regions, Belgium, Switzerland, Québec, other), not free text.
> - Open for you and Rebecca: may a per-challenge grid score ever be shown; how "un atelier par mois" in Complet works and its cap; Rétor's tone values and persona; the v1 definition of done and the Arena threshold (ch. 18).
