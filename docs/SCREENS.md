# Screens

The validated mockup (`Brainstorming/App LEQ (1).html`, third version, reviewed by Rebecca) has 50 iPhone screens in 9 flows. This file maps every screen to a route in the mobile app, to the phase that builds it, and to its current status. Update the status column in the same commit as the screen.

Route convention: Expo Router paths under `apps/mobile/src/app`. Route groups in parentheses are not part of the URL. Proposed layout:

- `(accueil)`: the arrival flow, no tab bar, shown while the person has no account or has not finished the diagnostic.
- `(onglets)`: the tab bar. Tabs: Aujourd'hui, L'Arène (present only when `drapeaux.arene` or `drapeaux.duels` is on), Défis, Progrès, Moi.
- Everything else is a stack screen pushed over the tabs (brief, recording, sending, feedback, duel, debate, offers).

Several mockup screens are states of one route, not separate routes (C1, C2 and C3 are the Arena tab in three states; X3 is X2 after a failure; B5b is B5 with the flags off). They keep their own row so that nothing in the mockup is lost, and the route column says "state of".

Status values: not started, placeholder, in progress, done. "Placeholder" means the route exists with the final strings but no logic (used in Phase 0 for A1 and B1). "Done" means built with typecheck, lint and tests green; whether a screen has been tapped through on a device is tracked in docs/STATUS.md ("Verified versus unverified").

Two further observations from reading the mockup against the contract, recorded here so they are not lost:

- D1 "Tes cinq voix" (Structure, Présence, Clarté, Rythme, Silences) and H2 "Grille de Rebecca" name criteria that do not exist yet. They are placeholders until Rebecca's grid arrives; the progress screen reads its axes from `criteres_grille`.
- A6 and D1 name a speaker archetype ("Le Conteur"). The cahier does not define archetypes. Whether the profile has a name, and which names, is an input from Rebecca (docs/OPEN-INPUTS.md). Until then A6 shows measures and levers without an archetype title.
